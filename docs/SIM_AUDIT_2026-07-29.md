## A1 (found and FIXED 2026-07-30) — consumables were used with no target, so every card-modifying tarot was a no-op

Found by `scripts/probe_fix_reachability.py` while interpreting a flat A/B: 28% of
episodes used a tarot, yet **0%** of episodes had a single modified card in the
deck. That is impossible if tarots were landing.

`card_selection.apply_action` receives the agent's selected card `subset` and
throws it away for consumables (`card_selection.py:217-218`):

```python
elif intent == INTENT_USE_CONSUMABLE:
    return {"type": "use_consumable", "consumable_idx": 0, "target_cards": []}
```

`env_v7` duplicates the same logic in two more places (`env_v7.py:294`, `:429`).

Verified consequences:
- `apply_tarot` computes `targets` from `target_indices or []` → empty, so
  Justice/Devil/Magician/Empress/Hierophant/Lovers/Strength/Death/Hanged Man and
  the suit-changers **do nothing** — and still return `True`, so the consumable is
  consumed. The agent pays a card and gets nothing. Measured: modified cards
  `0 -> 0` with `target_cards=[]`, `0 -> 1` with `target_cards=[0]`.
- `consumable_idx` is hardcoded to `0`, so consumable **slot 1 is unreachable**
  for the entire project history.

**This makes the C7 deck-persistence fix unreachable by the PPO agent.** The
permanent deck is now correct, but nothing in the action space can modify it, so
there is nothing to persist. Purple seals (C6), Steel (C3) and Glass (H2) are all
gated behind the same thing.

This is Ceiling 2 (action-space expressiveness) from the retrospective, in a place
the V6 audit never looked. It also invalidates any "the agent used a tarot"
statistic in the V7 run logs — those tarots did nothing.

### The fix

`N_INTENTS` 3 → 4: one intent per consumable slot
(`USE_CONSUMABLE_0` / `USE_CONSUMABLE_1`), and the selected card subset is now
forwarded as `target_cards`. The intent mask enables a slot only when it holds a
consumable. The shop's "use planet" action now scans every slot instead of only
slot 0.

Fixing it surfaced three further places that assumed three intents and no
consumable subset — all in `train_v7.py`, all now driven off a shared
`SUBSET_INTENTS` constant so adding an intent cannot silently miss a site:

1. `intent_counts = [0, 0, 0]` — immediate IndexError. Harmless.
2. Subset sampling gated on `intent in (INTENT_PLAY, INTENT_DISCARD)` in three
   places. Consumables would have emitted no subset and the target list would
   have been empty **again** — the fix would have appeared applied while
   changing nothing.
3. The PPO update zeroed subset log-prob and entropy for consumable steps. The
   behaviour policy now includes that term in `total_log_prob`, so zeroing it at
   update time desynchronises the importance ratio. This would not crash and no
   test would fail — it would quietly corrupt the gradient for every consumable
   action.

### Measured effect

Reachability probe, n=150, on a checkpoint trained for **400 iterations** (versus
2,260 for the pre-fix numbers, so with ~5x *less* training):

| precondition | before A1 | after A1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| any modified card in deck | 0.7% | **20.0%** |
| any Steel card → C3 | 0% | 1.3% |
| any Glass card → H2 | 0% | 3.3% |
| any Purple seal → C6 | 0% | 0.7% |

The 0.7% "before" figure came from booster packs adding pre-enhanced cards, not
from tarots landing. C7, C3, H2 and C6 are now reachable by a learning agent.

Covered by `tests/test_consumable_targeting.py` (18 tests); 6 of them fail if the
empty-target dispatch is reintroduced.

## CRITICAL — changes optimal play or breaks whole archetypes

### C1. No money is awarded for beating a blind

`game.py:538-542`:
```python
earnings = self.hands_left * HAND_PAYOUT      # $1 per unused hand
interest = min(self.dollars // INTEREST_RATE, INTEREST_CAP)
self.dollars += earnings + interest
```

Real Balatro also pays a base blind reward: **Small $3, Big $4, Boss $5,
Showdown Boss $8**. The sim pays none of it.

Two consequences, both large:

1. **The agent runs at roughly 40% of real income.** Ante 1 small blind,
   2 hands left, $4 banked: real = $3+$2+$0 = $5, sim = $2. This is almost
   certainly the root cause of V5's twelve shop-starvation failures, and the
   reason `R_EMPTY_SLOT_PER_ANTE` had to be invented to bully the agent into
   buying anything.
2. **It biases toward exactly the greedy play we want to eliminate.** Unused
   hands are ~40% of round income in the real game but **100% of it here**.
   So clearing a blind on hand 1 and banking three hands is *economically
   correct in this sim*. That's a second, independent force pushing the same
   behavior as `R_CARD_QUALITY` — reward shaping alone was never going to
   overcome it.

### C2. `ctx.all_cards` is the played cards — but jokers read it as three different things

`game.py:436` passes `all_cards=selected`, i.e. the cards just played.
Jokers were written against three incompatible meanings:

| Joker | File | Expects | Actually gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baron (×1.5 per King) | `scaling.py:753-758` | Kings **held in hand** | Kings among played cards |
| Shoot the Moon (+13/Queen) | `chips.py:333-336` | Queens **held in hand** | Queens among played cards |
| Steel Joker | `chips.py:153` | Steel cards in **full deck** | Steel among played cards |
| Stone Joker | `misc.py:239` | Stone cards in **full deck** | Stone among played cards |
| Glass Joker | `scaling.py:349` | Glass cards in **full deck** | Glass among played cards |
| Driver's License (≥16 enhanced) | `chips.py:339` | enhanced in **full deck** | enhanced among ≤5 played |

This breaks the entire **hold-cards** family (Baron/Mime/Shoot the Moon) and
the entire **deck-enhancement** family in one shot. Driver's License can never
fire (needs 16 from a pool of ≤5).

### C3. There is no held-in-hand scoring phase at all

The wiki specifies the order: played cards score → **then** held cards are
checked left-to-right for in-hand abilities → then independent jokers and
joker editions. The sim has no such phase, so held-in-hand effects were
retrofitted into the played-card loop:

`scoring.py:40-41`:
```python
elif card.enhancement == "Steel":
    ctx.mult_mult *= 1.5  # applies while held in hand; here approximated per scoring pass
```

Real Steel is **×1.5 while held in hand** and gives *nothing* when played.
The sim inverts it. Same root cause as C2, C5, and the Blue Seal bug.
`j_mime` carries `# TODO: requires held_in_hand tracking` (`chips.py:121-122`)
though it does register a `pre_score` hook.

### C4. Joker order has no effect on the arithmetic

`scoring.py:139-141`:
```python
total_chips = base_chips + ctx.chips
total_mult  = (base_mult + ctx.mult) * ctx.mult_mult
score = int(total_chips * max(total_mult, 0))
```

All additive mult is pooled, then all ×mult is applied at the end. The sim
therefore always grants the **best possible ordering** for free.

The wiki is explicit that real Balatro applies jokers sequentially left-to-right,
so a `+Mult` joker placed after an `×Mult` joker adds to the already-multiplied
value. Their worked example, same two jokers, different order:
`40 × ((4+4) × 2) = 640` vs `40 × ((4 × 2) + 4) = 480`.

Implications:
- Sim scores are **inflated** relative to real Balatro.
- `auto_position_on_buy` (`synergy.py:399`) is arithmetically pointless.
- "Joker positioning as an explicit action" — listed in the retrospective's
  *What I Never Tried* — is **unimplementable** without restructuring this.
- Joker editions are applied in a batch at the end (`scoring.py:129-135`)
  rather than at each joker's position. Wiki: Foil is "+50 Chips directly
  before the Joker is reached"; Polychrome is "×1.5 Mult directly after."

### C5. Gold Seal is inverted

`game.py:565-568` pays $3 per gold-sealed card **held in hand at end of round**.
Real Gold Seal: "**Earn $3 when this card is played and scores.**"

(The Gold *enhancement* at `game.py:570-573` — $3 per Gold card held at end of
round — is **correct**. The two got swapped in spirit.)

### C6. Purple and Blue Seals fire on the wrong events

Wiki-confirmed, carrying over from the 2026-07-28 session:
- **Purple Seal** — sim fires on *played* cards (`game.py:483-485`). Real:
  "Creates a Tarot card **when discarded**." `_discard` (`game.py:740`) has no
  seal handling at all.
- **Blue Seal** — sim fires on scoring cards after a play (`game.py:475-481`).
  Real: "Creates the Planet card for final played poker hand of round **if held
  in hand**" — an end-of-round, held-in-hand effect.

### C7. Deck reset to vanilla every blind

`game.py:242` → `_init_deck()` → `make_standard_deck()`. See
`V9_BRAINSTORM_2026-07.md` Defect 1. Still the single largest issue.

## HIGH — wrong numbers or missing mechanics

### H1. Lucky Card probability is wrong
`scoring.py:34-39` uses `1/4` for the +20 Mult roll. Real is **1 in 5**.
The inline comment asserts *"real Balatro: 1 in 4 chance"* — mis-transcribed
with a confident wrong comment, which is how it survived the V6 audit. The
$20-at-1/15 roll is correct. Net: Lucky cards are 25% stronger than real.

### H2. Glass cards are never destroyed
Real: "X2 Mult when scored", with a **1 in 4 chance to be destroyed after all
scoring is finished." The sim implements the ×2 (`scoring.py:32-33`) and no
destruction anywhere. Glass becomes a free ×2 with no downside.

Knock-on: real Glass Joker gains ×0.75 Mult **per Glass card destroyed**. With
no destruction possible, the sim substitutes "per Glass card in deck"
(`scaling.py:346-349`) — a different joker wearing the same name.

### H3. Two RNG sites bypass `game.rng`
`scoring.py:35-38` (Lucky) and `mult.py:377` (Space Joker) call the global
`random` module. Breaks seed reproducibility in `balatro-rl`. The mcts repo
claims "~50 sites" were routed through `game.rng` — **verify it caught these
two**, since determinism is load-bearing for MCTS cloning.

### H4. Boss blinds: ~half are no-ops, several are wrong, and Ante 8 is too easy

`game.py:205`: `boss_key = self.rng.choice(BOSS_BLINDS[:20])` — for **every**
ante.

- **Ante 8 never gets a Showdown boss.** Real Balatro always uses one at
  Ante 8: Violet Vessel (**6× base chips**), Crimson Heart (disables a random
  Joker every hand), Verdant Leaf (all cards debuffed until a Joker is sold),
  Amber Acorn (flips/shuffles Jokers), Cerulean Bell (forces a card selected).
  None are implemented. **The sim's final boss is materially easier than the
  real game's**, at the exact point where runs are decided.
- **Implemented:** manacle, needle, water, goad, window, head, plant, fish,
  psychic, hook, eye, mouth.
- **In the pool but no effect:** wall (4× chips), flint (halve base chips and
  mult), tooth (−$1 per card played), arm (−1 hand level), ox (money → $0),
  house, wheel, mark, pillar, serpent. So a large fraction of boss blinds are
  just a 2×-target blind with no gimmick.
- **Wrong effects** (comments in `game.py:97-122` vs wiki):
  | Key | Sim comment | Real effect |
  |---|---|---|
  | `bl_goad` | all **Clubs** debuffed | all **Spades** debuffed |
  | `bl_wall` | +100 chips to target | **4×** base chips |
  | `bl_house` | odd ranks debuffed | first hand drawn face down |
  | `bl_ox` | playing **Flush** → $0 | playing **most-used hand type** → $0 |
  | `bl_verdant` | −1 discard until Spade scored | all cards debuffed until a Joker is sold |
  | `bl_amber` | hand full of Twos | flips and shuffles all Jokers |
  | `bl_cerulean` | first hand decided by Blue Seal | forces 1 card always selected |
  | `bl_serpent` | discard hand, draw new | always draw **3** cards after play/discard |
  | `bl_pillar` | previously-played cards score nothing | cards played in Small/Big Blind are debuffed |

  Note **The Club** (debuffs Clubs) doesn't exist in the sim — `bl_goad` was
  implemented as The Club and labeled The Goad.
- `bl_needle` sets `hands_left = 1` (`game.py:291`) but keeps the standard 2×
  boss target. Real Needle requires only **1×**. Needle is far harder here
  than in the real game.

### H5. `HAND_LEVEL_MULT["Flush Five"] = 6`, real is +3
`constants.py:52`. Low impact (rare hand), but notable because the file's
docstring claims *"All values verified against game source."* The other 23
values in that table are correct.

### H6. Skipping a blind pays a flat $5 instead of granting a Tag
`game.py:607-608`, documented as an approximation. Tags (Investment, Voucher,
Charm, Double...) are a real strategic axis; reducing them to $5 makes skipping
a pure economic decision.

## MEDIUM

### M1. `j_supernova` is buyable with no implementation
1 of 144 catalogue jokers is a literal no-op purchase.

### M2. Two utility jokers are unobtainable but have live passive code
`game.py:225-228` applies passives for `j_juggler` (+1 hand size) and
`j_drunkard` (+1 discard), but **neither is in the shop catalogue**, so the code
can never run. `j_stone_joker` is likewise registered but unbuyable.
(`j_troubadour` and `j_merry_andy` *are* buyable — those two work.)

### M3. 25 registered-but-unbuyable joker keys
Mostly dead aliases (`j_greedy` / `j_greedy_joker` vs the actually-buyable
`j_greedy_mult`). Two are **different implementations of the same joker**
(`j_space` → `_Space`, `j_space_joker` → `_SpaceJoker`), so there's a live risk
the dead one is the correct one. Worth one reconciliation pass.

### M4. The card-quality reward's baseline can silently saturate
`_best_hand_score` (`env_v7.py:484-540`) scores only the **top 3 hand-type
priority tiers, capped at 8 combos**. A heavily-leveled low hand type — a
Mercury-spammed Pair, exactly what a pair build produces — falls outside those
tiers and never gets scored. Since `quality_ratio = min(played/best, 1.0)`, the
term pins to 1.0 and stops discriminating.

It doesn't *punish* the right play, but `R_CARD_QUALITY` — the reward term the
retrospective calls most impactful — **goes blind precisely for the archetype
we now want to target.**

## Revised priority order

The C-tier items reinforce the `V9_BRAINSTORM` thesis rather than complicating
it. **C1 and C2 are new and cheap**, and C1 in particular is a one-line-ish fix
that independently biased the agent toward the greedy play.

1. **C1 (blind reward money)** — smallest diff, largest behavioral change.
   Fix before anything else and re-measure; it may move results on its own.
2. **C7 (deck persistence) + C6/C5 (seal triggers)** — the deck-building
   unlock.
3. **C2 (`all_cards` split)** — separate into `played_cards`, `held_cards`,
   `full_deck` and fix the six jokers. Unlocks Baron/Mime and deck-scaling.
4. **C3 (held-in-hand phase)** — depends on C2. Unlocks Steel properly.
5. **H1, H2, H5** — numeric one-liners, do them together.
6. **H4 (boss blinds)** — biggest remaining chunk. At minimum add Showdown
   bosses for Ante 8 and fix `bl_goad`'s suit; the unimplemented gimmicks can
   land incrementally.
7. **C4 (sequential joker scoring)** — the largest refactor. Defer until the
   above are done, but it gates any work on joker positioning, and it means
   **every score this project has ever logged is inflated**.

Do NOT trust any pre-fix win-rate comparison after C1 or C7 lands — the
economy and the strategy space both change, so 2.35% is not a valid baseline
for the fixed sim. Re-baseline with the scripted player.
